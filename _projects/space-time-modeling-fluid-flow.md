title: Space–time reduced-order modeling of fluid flow
description: Three-dimensional convolutions for forecasting fluid flow beyond a training window.

category: PhD research
---

We built a reduced-order model that learns how flow structures evolve in space and time. Its three-dimensional convolutional kernels operate on sequences of flow snapshots to learn those patterns jointly. We evaluated the model on fluid dynamics problems and tested how well it extrapolates beyond the training data. The article was selected as an Editor’s Pick in *Physics of Fluids*.

[Physics of Fluids article](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0145071)
