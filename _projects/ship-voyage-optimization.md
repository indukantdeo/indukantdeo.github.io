We investigated how ships can adjust their speed along a fixed route to reduce underwater noise exposure while accounting for fuel use and voyage constraints. The work contributed to MUTE-DSS, a decision support system that couples acoustic modeling with ship voyage optimization.

[Voyage optimization paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oceaneng.2024.118687) · [MUTE-DSS paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oceaneng.2025.123343)
