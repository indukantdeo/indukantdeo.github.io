We trained a convolutional network to predict transmission loss fields from range-dependent bathymetry. The model computes a field in a single step and uses replay-based continual learning to adapt as it encounters new seabed profiles. We evaluated it on several test cases, including a benchmark around Dickins Seamount.

[Journal of the Acoustical Society of America article](https://doi.org/10.1121/10.0036773) · [Open preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08091)
