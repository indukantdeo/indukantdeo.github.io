This example walks through a finite element solution of the one-dimensional heat equation. It covers mesh construction, assembly of the global mass and stiffness matrices, Dirichlet boundary conditions, and implicit Euler time stepping with JAX.

[Code and notebooks on GitHub](https://github.com/indukantdeo/1D_FEM_JAX)
