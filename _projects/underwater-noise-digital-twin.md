We developed a probabilistic surrogate to estimate how sound propagates through complex coastal waters. The model combines a physics-based mean function, learned features for bathymetry and acoustic conditions, and a Gaussian process that estimates uncertainty. We demonstrated the approach along shipping routes in the Salish Sea.

[Ocean Engineering article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.oceaneng.2026.125461) · [Open preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25730)
