This notebook uses proper orthogonal decomposition (POD) to construct a reduced basis for the diffusion equation. Galerkin projection then produces a smaller system that evolves the solution in that basis.

[Code and notebook on GitHub](https://github.com/indukantdeo/pod-galerkin)
